Billions of records / a small desktop

SHADOW

I made 4.19 billion records of exposed login details searchable on a 16 GB Mac Mini. The system prepares its indexes in advance, so looking up a website’s domain doesn’t require reading the whole collection again.

03

A hidden flaw / a global fix

Google Cloud Armor

Some web requests were getting past Google Cloud Armor’s protection, even though their logged details didn’t explain why. With the GovTech Edu team, I spent months tracing the problem and helped produce a reproducible report. Google confirmed the cause and released a global patch.

The issue affected Cloud Armor protection on Classic Application Load Balancers.
