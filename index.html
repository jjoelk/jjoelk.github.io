SHADOW · Data engineering

Why repeat the work on every search?

SHADOW began with a narrower question: which two lookups did the system actually need? That decision shaped a design for 4.19 billion canonical credential records—records of exposed login details—on a 16 GB Mac Mini. One warm, single-threaded domain lookup took 0.38 seconds. The article follows the design, the mistakes it exposed, and the limits that remain.

Google Cloud Armor · Security investigation

The logs weren’t telling the whole story.

Cloud Armor’s logs made blocked and allowed requests look identical. Our investigation with the GovTech Edu team followed that discrepancy through months of work to a reproducible report, Google’s confirmation of an issue affecting Classic Application Load Balancers, and a global patch. The write-up follows how we worked through the missing explanation.
